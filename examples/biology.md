# Cell biology

## Mitosis
Cell division that produces two identical daughter cells.

## Osmosis
Movement of water across a semipermeable membrane.

### Organelles
- ATP: Energy currency of the cell
- DNA: Molecule that stores genetic information

Q: What is a ribosome?
A: The structure that builds proteins.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Enzyme | A protein that speeds up a reaction |
